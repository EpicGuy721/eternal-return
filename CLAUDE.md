- Worldgen vision and status (terrain, biomes, caves, scope, compatibility): see [docs/worldgen.md](docs/worldgen.md).
- The Eternal Return world preset files (settings preset, noise settings, world preset, preset category and tags) are generated by `tools/worldgen/build_preset.py` from `tools/worldgen/knobs.json`. Change the script or the knobs and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated JSON.
- Carving and decoration (ores, plants, structures) outside Eternal Return worlds must not change: the control runs prove it (src/gametest/resources/fingerprints: carved chunks in every control world, decorated chunks in the vanilla world, and no block of this mod in the decorated chunks of the Moderner Beta worlds, whose decoration does not repeat from run to run; checked by runAllGametests). Re-record them (-PrecordFingerprints) only for an intended change to those worlds.
- The ore variant resources (overlay textures, models, blockstates, loot tables, recipes, ore tags, the needs_*_tool tags, docs/worldgen-maps/ores) are generated by `tools/ores/build_ores.py` from `src/main/resources/eternalreturn/ore_variants.json` (which the game also reads to register the blocks) and the overlay images in `tools/ores/overlays/`. Change those and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The wool and concrete stairs and slabs (blockstates, models, loot tables, recipes, tags) and the layered carpets (vanilla carpet blockstates and loot tables, the carpet layer models) are generated by `tools/blocks/build_blocks.py`. Change the script and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The enchanted book recipes that scrolls teach (data/eternalreturn/recipe/enchanted_book) are generated by `tools/scrolls/build_scrolls.py` from `tools/scrolls/scrolls.json`. Change the table and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated recipes.
- The fish resources (item textures and models, the fish language lines, the habitat biome tags `fish_habitat/*`, the catch tables `gameplay/fishing/fish` and `gameplay/lava_fishing/fish`, the `raw_fish` and `minecraft:fishes` item tags, docs/fish.md) are generated by `tools/fish/build_fish.py` from `src/main/resources/eternalreturn/fish.json` (which the game also reads to register the fish and the fishermen's trades) and the textures in `tools/fish/textures/`. Change those and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The maple, pale oak and azalea wood sets (and the azalea tree feature), maple petals, the maple leaf particle, the autumnal forest's trees and features and the biome itself (`data/eternalreturn/worldgen/biome/autumnal_forest.json`), and the vanilla block and item tags they join (listed in `tools/woods/generated_tags.txt`) are generated by `tools/woods/build_woods.py` from vanilla's cherry set in the Loom cache. Change the script and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files. The textures are hand-made and live in `assets/eternalreturn/textures`.
- The copper and rose gold gear resources (item models, recipes, the worn armor and horse armor textures, the vanilla item tags they join, listed in `tools/materials/generated_tags.txt`) and gold armor's replacement worn textures are generated by `tools/materials/build_materials.py` from vanilla's iron gear and the gold armor textures in `tools/materials/textures/`. Change those and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files. The item icons are hand-made and live in `assets/eternalreturn/textures/item`.
- The ruby and sapphire resources (items, blocks, base ores including the Nether ruby ore, their models, loot tables, recipes and shared c: tags) and Eternal Return's extra ore features (ruby, sapphire, the per-biome bonuses) are generated by `tools/gems/build_gems.py` from the icons in `tools/gems/textures/` and the ore overlays in `tools/ores/overlays/`. Run it before `tools/ores/build_ores.py`, which makes their host stone variants. Change those and rerun; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The birds' texture layers (`textures/entity/bird/<model>/`) are painted by `tools/birds/paint_birds.py`, and their model code (`client/BirdModels.java`), the bird language lines and docs/birds.md are written by `tools/birds/build_birds.py`, both from `src/main/resources/eternalreturn/birds.json` (which the game also reads: palette, shapes, patterns, presets) and the models `tools/models/bird_<model>.bbmodel`. Change those and rerun them; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The vanilla enchantments that go on more items (`data/minecraft/enchantment/`: the protections, unbreaking and frost_walker for horse armor; knockback, looting, sharpness, smite, bane_of_arthropods and fire_aspect for spears), their item tags (`eternalreturn:enchantable/*_or_*`) and the additions of spears and the speargun to vanilla's enchantable tags (`data/minecraft/tags/item/enchantable/`) are generated by `tools/enchantments/build_enchantments.py` from vanilla's definitions in the Loom cache. Change the script and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The Sandown's texture (`textures/entity/sandown.png`) is painted by `tools/mobs/paint_sandown.py` to the box-UV layout of `tools/models/sandown.bbmodel`. Change the script and rerun it; do not hand-edit the texture.
- The item frame resources (the dyed frame block models and their grey backs, the glass frames' block models and backs, the frame item models, vanilla's item_frame and glow_item_frame item models, and the two-layer frame icons `textures/item/*item_frame_border` and `*item_frame_inside`) are generated by `tools/frames/build_frames.py` from vanilla's frame models and textures in the Loom cache and the hand-made glass frame textures in `tools/frames/textures/`. Change those and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The trough resources (blockstates, block and item models, the content surface models and the carrot and seed pile textures, loot tables, recipes, the `eternalreturn:troughs` tags and `minecraft:mineable/axe`) are generated by `tools/troughs/build_troughs.py` from vanilla's textures in the Loom cache. Its wood list must match `Troughs.WOODS`. Change the script and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
- The dyed redstone lamp and dyed water cauldron resources (the lamp's textures and grey glass layers, block models, blockstates, vanilla's redstone_lamp item model, loot tables) are generated by `tools/lamps/build_lamps.py` from the hand-made lamp textures and their colour maps in `tools/lamps/textures/`. Change those and rerun it; do not hand-edit the generated files.
