# Fish

Generated by `tools/fish/build_fish.py` from `src/main/resources/eternalreturn/fish.json`; do not edit by hand.

53 fish: 45 caught in water with chum or glistering melon, and 8 caught in lava in the Nether with magma cream. All of them fill like raw salmon and can't be cooked.

A fish bites only where all its conditions hold. Its weight in the catch table comes from its rarity (common 30, uncommon 12, rare 5, epic 2, legendary 1; vanilla's cod, salmon, tropical fish and pufferfish together 100), so in any one spot the odds depend on which other fish bite there too. Glistering melon doubles the odds of rare, epic and legendary water fish.

Fishermen buy each fish from the level of its rarity (common 1, uncommon 2, rare 3, epic 4, legendary 5), every fish of that rarity at once rather than a random two. A fisherman only buys the freshwater fish of his own region (his biome: plains, desert, savanna, snow, taiga, jungle or swamp); every fisherman buys fish from the sea, cherry groves, underground water and the Nether. From novice on, every fisherman also sells bait: chum (16 for 1 emerald), glistering melon slice (4 for 1 emerald), magma cream (4 for 1 emerald).
