# Accent lists (approved by the owner, 2026-11-11)

Phase 7, batch 2 of worldgen expansion 2. Each patch (256 blocks a side) rolls one accent from its biome's list, and that accent turns up about 8 times across the patch (the owner's call). Accents are built only from blocks the biome already has. Peaks, edges and rivers take their patch's accent. The seas get them too (the owner's call). The game reads the lists from `data/eternalreturn/eternalreturn/accents.json`, which is the one to tune; this page is the record of what was approved.

## The accents

| Accent | What it is |
|---|---|
| Pond | A small pool (4 to 7 across, 1 to 2 deep) edged with the biome's own ground and plants: lily pads where the biome has them, sugar cane by sandy edges |
| Frozen pond | The same pond, iced over, with snow on its banks |
| Boulder ring | Five to nine of the biome's boulders in a loose ring, 8 to 12 across |
| Mossy boulders | A cluster of boulders patched with moss and mossy cobblestone, like vanilla's old growth taiga rocks |
| Ice boulders | Boulders of packed ice half sunk in the snow |
| Fallen log | One of the biome's own trees lying on its side, 4 to 9 long, mossy and with mushrooms in wet biomes |
| Snag | A dead standing trunk of the biome's wood, no leaves, sometimes broken off |
| Mushroom ring | A fairy ring of brown and red mushrooms, 5 to 9 across |
| Mossy clearing | A patch of moss blocks and moss carpet on the forest floor |
| Fern pocket | A thick cluster of ferns and large ferns |
| Flower pocket | A dense cluster of the patch's own flowers (from its palette) |
| Berry thicket | A tangle of sweet berry bushes |
| Petal drift | A carpet of the biome's petals (pink petals, maple petals) |
| Podzol patch | A spread of podzol and coarse dirt on the forest floor |
| Snow drift | A long low bank of snow layers |
| Stone outcrop | A rough spire of the biome's own stone breaking through the ground, 3 to 6 high |
| Hoodoo | A thin spire of the biome's terracotta bands, 4 to 8 high, with a wider cap |
| Dry wash | A winding dry streambed of sand or red sand and gravel, dead bushes along it |
| Bleached bones | A rib cage and spine of bone blocks half buried in the sand |
| Termite mound | A tall lumpy mound of coarse dirt |
| Melon patch | A few melons among the undergrowth |
| Pumpkin cluster | A few pumpkins together |

## The seas

On the sea floor, made only from what the seas already have (their floors, kelp, seagrass, coral, sea pickles, clay, and the driftwood the coasts already wash up).

| Accent | What it is |
|---|---|
| Kelp ring | A ring of tall kelp columns round a bare patch of floor, 8 to 12 across |
| Sunken log | A waterlogged log lying on the floor (the wood the coast's driftwood is, or mangrove in the lagoon), seagrass around it |
| Seagrass bed | A dense patch of tall seagrass |
| Sand ripples | Bands of gravel and sand across the floor |
| Clay patch | A patch of clay in the floor |
| Sea boulders | A few boulders of the floor's stone, the same boulder ring as on land |
| Sea pickle cluster | Sea pickles in groups of three and four on a little rise |
| Coral head | A rounded mound of coral blocks with coral and fans on it |
| Ice floe | A flat slab of packed ice floating on the surface |
