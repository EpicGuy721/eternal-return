# Birds

Generated by `tools/birds/build_birds.py` from `src/main/resources/eternalreturn/birds.json`; do not hand-edit.

A bird is one mob with a shape, a base colour and up to two patterns, each in its own colour (the second drawn over the first). 90 percent of birds are one of the presets below; the rest are random. The presets, rendered: [birds/presets.png](birds/presets.png).

## Shapes

Birds fly off from a player within 6 blocks (half that if they sneak). A shape that perches flies up to rest on leaves and logs; the others stay low (ducks swim). Flocking shapes take off together when one is startled (or now and then on their own) and fly as a flock by the boids rules; loose ones hold together less; solo ones fly alone.
